What is VCF (vCard)?

VCF (also known as vCard) is a standard file format for storing contact information, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, company names, and more.

It is the only format supported by nearly all mobile phones for bulk importing contacts at once.

Why Convert Excel/CSV to VCF?

If you have a long list of phone numbers in Excel or CSV format, you cannot directly import them into your phone. Mobile phones only recognize VCF files for batch contact import.

Converting your list to VCF allows you to import hundreds or thousands of contacts in one click without typing them manually.

How to Bulk Import Contacts to Your Phone

  1. Prepare your contacts in Excel or CSV (Name + Phone required)
