About Random Wheel Spinner

Random Wheel Spinner is a free, simple, and interactive decision-making tool that helps you choose randomly between multiple options. Whether you need to make a quick choice, pick a winner, decide a game result, or settle a small debate, this wheel gives you a fair and fun result.

You can fully customize the wheel with your own text options, update the wheel in real time, and spin to get a random result instantly.

How to Use the Random Wheel

  1. Enter your custom options in the input boxes (e.g. activities, names, choices)
  2. Add or remove options using the + and ✕ buttons
  3. Click "Update Wheel" to refresh your spinning wheel
  4. Press "SPIN THE WHEEL" to start the random spin
  5. The wheel will rotate and stop at a random result automatically

Common Uses of the Random Wheel

  • Make daily decisions (what to eat, what to do, where to go)
  • Pick winners for giveaways, contests, and raffles
  • Choose games, activities, or challenges
  • Randomly select students or participants in class
  • Create fun challenges for friends and family
  • Help with indecision in a fair and random way

Key Features

  • Smooth 3D-style spinning animation
  • Bright, colorful wheel segments
  • Works on mobile, tablet, and desktop
